# Crimson Room Walkthrough Notes

Source-backed coverage status: `partial_verified`.

This file intentionally avoids unsourced full walkthrough reconstruction. It records the verified progression fragments currently available in the local knowledge base.

## Opening State

The player wakes in an unfamiliar crimson room and must escape by inspecting fixed camera angles, interacting with objects, collecting items, and using those items on compatible room objects.

## Verified Object Chain

1. Inspect the bed area and lift the pillow to find the golden key.
2. Use the golden key on a previously locked desk drawer.
3. Collect the power cord from that drawer.
4. Use the power cord on the stereo on top of the desk.
5. Turn on the stereo after connecting power.
6. Later, after the room's puzzles have been solved, collect the screwdriver.
7. Use the screwdriver on the locked door's doorknob to escape.
