# Myst - Partial Walkthrough Notes

Coverage: high-level verified flow, not exact per-Age solutions.

1. Treat Myst Island as the hub.
2. Read journals and clues before operating mechanisms.
3. Use revealed linking books to travel to Ages.
4. In each Age, solve its local puzzle chain and recover pages.
5. Be careful with the red, blue, and green books; the ending depends on which book you trust and what page you bring.
